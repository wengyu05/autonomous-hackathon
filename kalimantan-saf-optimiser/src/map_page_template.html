    About this map

    • Site and airport coordinates come straight from the organisers' files, unchanged.
    • The stars are a hand estimate of where refineries will probably go, at the biomass-weighted centre of each region. They are not solver output. The optimisation model will choose the real sites.
    • Coastline and borders: Natural Earth 1:10m (public domain). It is accurate to about 1–2 km. For example, Balikpapan airport, which sits on the shore, shows as km offshore in this data.
    • Distances on the map are straight-line. Roads in Kalimantan are longer.
